# 06 — Typography System

## Typography Philosophy

Typography is not used to communicate information.

Typography is used to create emotion.

Words are part of the landscape.

They occupy space.

They create rhythm.

They create silence.

The visitor should often understand the emotion before reading the sentence.

Typography is architecture.

Not decoration.

---

# The Three Levels

The entire landing only uses three levels of typography.

Nothing else.

## Level 01 — Manifesto

Purpose:

Emotion.

Examples:

Not every destination is meant for everyone.

The internet tells everyone where to go.

MindRoute starts with you.

Characteristics:

Very large.

Confident.

Editorial.

Minimal.

Usually no more than 4–8 words.

Never explanatory.

Never promotional.

Appears rarely.

Every appearance must feel important.

---

## Level 02 — Narrative

Purpose:

Guide the visitor.

Expand the previous thought.

Characteristics:

One or two short paragraphs.

Maximum 2–3 lines.

Highly readable.

Elegant.

Never overwhelming.

Acts as breathing room between emotional moments.

---

## Level 03 — Interface

Purpose:

Support interaction.

Labels.

Navigation.

Metadata.

Buttons.

Characteristics:

Small.

Quiet.

Secondary.

Never competes with emotional typography.

Almost invisible.

---

# Hierarchy

The emotional headline is always dominant.

Everything else supports it.

Never create visual competition.

The visitor should instantly know what matters.

---

# Line Length

Never use long paragraphs.

Every sentence should feel intentional.

Ideal width:

40–65 characters.

Text should never resemble an article.

It should resemble poetry.

---

# White Space

Typography needs air.

Never compress text.

Large margins.

Large spacing.

Large breathing room.

Luxury typography is never crowded.

---

# Alignment

Alignment communicates emotion.

Left alignment creates honesty.

Centered alignment creates ceremony.

Right alignment creates tension.

Use all three intentionally.

Never default to center for every section.

Avoid predictable layouts.

---

# Scale

Think in dramatic contrasts.

Tiny labels.

Huge statements.

Very little in between.

The visitor should constantly perceive hierarchy.

---

# Editorial Principles

Typography should feel closer to:

Kinfolk

Monocle

Aesop

Apartamento

Apple Editorial

Not:

Startup landing pages

Corporate SaaS

Marketing websites

Blog articles

---

# Motion Behaviour

Typography never appears.

It emerges.

Letters may separate.

Words may drift.

Entire sentences may slowly assemble.

The movement is subtle.

Elegant.

Confident.

Never flashy.

Never kinetic typography.

Never excessive.

---

# Scroll Behaviour

Typography belongs to the environment.

It should move with the world.

Sometimes slower.

Sometimes faster.

Sometimes remaining fixed while the environment changes.

Never simply scrolling with the document.

---

# Contrast

Do not rely only on font weight.

Create hierarchy using:

Scale.

Position.

Space.

Light.

Opacity.

Rhythm.

Hierarchy should feel natural.

Not forced.

---

# Emotional Weight

Different typography creates different emotions.

Huge.

Slow.

Calm.

Creates reflection.

Small.

Precise.

Creates trust.

Bold.

Rare.

Creates emphasis.

Silence.

Creates luxury.

---

# Writing Style

Every sentence should feel inevitable.

Avoid:

Marketing.

Buzzwords.

Sales language.

Product language.

Corporate language.

Replace explanations with observations.

Instead of:

"Our AI understands your personality."

Write:

"We begin with who you are."

Instead of:

"Our algorithm evolves over time."

Write:

"Every journey changes the next."

---

# Typography + Photography

Never separate them.

Words should belong inside the image.

Not above it.

Not below it.

They should feel discovered inside the world.

The image and the typography should complete one another.

---

# Typography + Motion

Typography should never animate independently.

The world changes first.

Typography emerges from the transformation.

The visitor should feel the sentence appearing because the environment revealed it.

Not because the browser animated it.

---

# Typography + Network

Whenever possible,
the network should interact with typography.

Connections may travel behind letters.

Nodes may illuminate words.

Lines may subtly frame sentences.

Typography and the network belong to the same visual system.

---

# Typography Density

Less is always more.

If a sentence can lose three words,
remove them.

If a paragraph can become one sentence,
rewrite it.

If an idea can become four words,
make it four words.

Confidence speaks quietly.

---

# Forbidden

Never:

ALL CAPS paragraphs.

Gradient text.

Outlined text.

Neon effects.

Heavy shadows.

Animated text for the sake of animation.

Large blocks of copy.

Long feature descriptions.

Marketing slogans.

Generic startup headlines.

---

# References

Study the typography of:

Apple

Aesop

Nothing

Kinfolk Magazine

Monocle

The New York Times Magazine

Apartamento

Stripe Sessions

Notice how typography creates atmosphere before information.

That is the goal.

---

# Final Principle

If all photography disappeared,
the typography should still feel beautiful.

If all typography disappeared,
the photography should still tell the story.

Neither should depend on the other.

Together,
they should become inseparable.
