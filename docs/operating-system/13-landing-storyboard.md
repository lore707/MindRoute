# 13 — Landing Storyboard

## Philosophy

The landing is one uninterrupted cinematic experience.

Not a website.

Not a sequence of sections.

The visitor should feel like they have travelled before they even start planning a journey.

Every scene exists because the previous one emotionally requires the next.

Nothing feels inserted.

Everything feels inevitable.

---

# Scene 01 — The Question

## Emotion

Curiosity.

Silence.

Wonder.

## Goal

Challenge the visitor's biggest assumption.

Travel should not begin with destinations.

## Visual

Almost complete darkness.

Very slow atmospheric movement.

Tiny floating particles.

A faint identity network slowly breathing.

One enormous editorial sentence.

> Not every destination is meant for everyone.

Almost nothing else exists.

A single understated CTA.

The visitor should pause.

Not click.

---

# Scene 02 — The Truth

## Emotion

Recognition.

## Goal

Reveal the invisible problem.

Algorithms quietly shape our desires.

## Visual

Instagram posts.

TikToks.

Pinterest boards.

Travel blogs.

Destination lists.

Everything slowly converges.

The same cities begin appearing repeatedly.

Tokyo.

Bali.

Santorini.

Iceland.

The feeds become visually repetitive.

Eventually only one destination remains.

The visitor realizes:

"We all want the same places."

No explanation is necessary.

The visuals communicate everything.

---

# Scene 03 — Reflection

## Emotion

Self-awareness.

## Goal

Shift attention from destinations to identity.

## Visual

The overwhelming destination feed slowly dissolves.

Darkness returns.

The network begins reconnecting.

One sentence appears.

> Before asking where to go,
> maybe we should ask who is travelling.

The world becomes quiet again.

---

# Scene 04 — The Philosophy

## Emotion

Hope.

## Goal

Introduce MindRoute.

Not as AI.

As a different way of deciding.

## Visual

The identity network gently expands.

Nodes illuminate.

Connections grow.

Destinations emerge organically from the network.

Not from search.

Not from maps.

From identity itself.

Minimal copy.

The visuals tell the story.

---

# Scene 05 — How MindRoute Thinks

## Emotion

Understanding.

## Goal

Explain the process without exposing technology.

## Visual

The network transforms into a flowing journey.

Identity.

↓

Needs.

↓

Mood.

↓

Experiences.

↓

Destination.

↓

Journey.

Everything feels alive.

No cards.

No diagrams.

Only transformation.

The visitor understands the logic intuitively.

---

# Scene 06 — Proof

## Emotion

Trust.

## Goal

Demonstrate personalization.

## Visual

One destination.

Lisbon.

The screen divides.

Two completely different people.

Two completely different journeys.

Same city.

Different lives.

Everything animates simultaneously.

Then reconnects.

The message becomes obvious.

Destinations never change.

People do.

---

# Scene 07 — Explainability

## Emotion

Confidence.

## Goal

Remove the mystery behind recommendations.

## Visual

One destination appears.

Kyoto.

Around it,

soft glowing connections emerge.

Nature.

Silence.

Walkability.

Previous journeys.

Creative curiosity.

Slow travel.

Each connection quietly illuminates.

Nothing feels technical.

Everything feels human.

---

# Scene 08 — Memory

## Emotion

Attachment.

## Goal

Show that every journey teaches something.

## Visual

A timeline slowly grows.

Portugal.

↓

Japan.

↓

Norway.

↓

Chile.

The identity network becomes richer.

Recommendations evolve.

The visitor understands:

MindRoute remembers.

Not destinations.

Me.

---

# Scene 09 — The Reveal

## Emotion

Satisfaction.

## Goal

Reveal the actual product.

## Visual

The interface finally appears.

Not suddenly.

It emerges naturally from the previous scene.

Maps.

Travel profile.

Identity graph.

Timeline.

Recommendations.

Everything already feels familiar.

Because the visitor already understands the philosophy.

The interface becomes confirmation.

Not discovery.

---

# Scene 10 — The Beginning

## Emotion

Commitment.

Calm.

Excitement.

## Goal

Invite the visitor to begin.

## Visual

The network quietly reconnects into one glowing point.

Darkness surrounds it.

The CTA appears.

Simple.

Confident.

> Discover your first journey.

No urgency.

No countdown.

No marketing pressure.

The visitor should feel ready.

Not persuaded.

---

# Scroll Rhythm

The landing follows a constant emotional rhythm.

Curiosity

↓

Recognition

↓

Reflection

↓

Hope

↓

Understanding

↓

Trust

↓

Attachment

↓

Confirmation

↓

Commitment

Every scene must move the visitor exactly one step forward.

Never skip an emotion.

---

# Scene Transitions

Scenes never replace one another.

They evolve.

The network survives.

Light survives.

Photography survives.

Particles survive.

Only meaning changes.

The visitor should rarely perceive where one scene ends.

---

# Interaction

The visitor should never interrupt the narrative.

Interaction should gently support exploration.

Hover reveals.

Scroll uncovers.

Click confirms.

Nothing distracts.

---

# Final Feeling

The visitor should reach the bottom of the landing believing one sentence:

"I didn't just discover a travel product.

I discovered a different way of choosing where my life goes next."

If that thought exists,

the landing has succeeded.
